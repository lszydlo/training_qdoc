Three ways back to draft

The wall is drawn as one straight line, but a version rarely travels it once. These are the loops.

  • Reverted from reviewThe owner takes the version back to resolve suggestions. A file changed afterwards loses its review marks and approvals; the others keep theirs.
  • Declined in approvalOne approver declines with a comment and the version goes back to the owner.
  • New draft of an effective QDocAn author opens the next version. The effective one stays in force until the new one reaches its own effective date.
